Cluckin Dashboard — QSR Data Platform

A multi-outlet quick-service restaurant brand was losing money to food wastage and couldn't see where. I built the pipeline and dashboards that made the problem visible per outlet — and actionable.

Problem

Cluckin operates across multiple outlets, but had no visibility into food wastage or inventory turnover at any of them. Stock data existed, scattered and inconsistent, but nothing rolled it up into a view anyone could act on.

Without an outlet-level picture, wastage was treated as a fixed cost of doing business rather than something with identifiable causes. There was no way to tell a structural problem from a one-off bad week, or a badly-stocked outlet from a well-run one.

Approach

I started with the data itself, building a cleaning pipeline to get the raw outlet records into a consistent, trustworthy shape. Reporting on messy inputs would have produced numbers nobody believed, and a dashboard nobody trusts is a dashboard nobody opens.

From there I segmented outlets by order frequency and inventory turnover. That was the key move: it separated outlets with genuinely different operating patterns, so high-volume and low-volume locations could be judged against the right benchmark instead of a single company-wide average that flattered some and unfairly flagged others.

What I built

  • A data-cleaning pipeline on Google Sheets, normalising inconsistent outlet-level inventory and order records into a reliable reporting base.
  • An outlet segmentation model grouping locations by order frequency and inventory turnover, so comparisons were like-for-like.
  • Looker Studio dashboards surfacing wastage and turnover per outlet and per segment — built for the operations team to read directly, not for an analyst to interpret for them.

Outcome

  • Reduced food wastage by 12% Wastage moved from an accepted cost to a tracked, managed metric with visible causes.
  • Outlet-level visibility, for the first time Operations could compare locations against the right peer group and see which needed intervention.

Tech used

  • Looker Studio
  • Google Sheets pipeline
  • Data cleaning
  • Segmentation analysis

AI-Assisted Fitness Coaching Platform

Fitness coaches face a hard trade-off: individualised plans that eat their week, or templates that scale but don't fit anyone. I prototyped a way to get both — AI drafts the plan, the coach still signs it off.

Open the live demo

Problem

Coaches were spending significant time manually building individualised plans for each client — time that doesn't scale and isn't billable in proportion to the effort. The alternative most of them fell back on was a one-size-fits-all template, which is fast but produces exactly the generic experience clients leave over.

The interesting constraint: a coach's value isn't just the plan, it's their specific training methodology. Any tool that generated plans from generic fitness knowledge would replace the coach's judgement with something blander than what they already had.

Approach

Rather than write a specification document and hand it over, I rapid-prototyped the tool directly. For a product whose whole question is "does the generated plan actually feel like this coach wrote it?", a working prototype answers that in a session; a spec document never can.

The design decision that followed: the AI generates a draft, not a deliverable. Every plan is reviewed and finalised by a human coach before it reaches a client. That keeps the coach's judgement — and accountability — in the loop on something as consequential as someone's training, while still removing the blank-page work that was consuming their time.

What I built

  • An AI plan generator that produces personalised plans from client intake data and the individual coach's training methodology — so output reflects how that coach actually works, not a generic model of fitness.
  • Three synced dashboards — user, coach and admin — on a Supabase backend, each seeing the same underlying state from its own perspective.
  • A human-in-the-loop review step: the AI draft goes to the coach, who edits and approves before anything is delivered to the client.

Outcome

  • Reduced plan-creation time Coaches start from a tailored draft instead of a blank page.
  • Individualised plans replaced generic templates The scalable option is now the personalised one, rather than the compromise.
  • Coach judgement preserved Nothing reaches a client without a human coach reviewing and finalising it.

Tech used

  • Supabase
  • LLM plan generation
  • Rapid prototyping
  • Multi-role dashboards
  • Human-in-the-loop design

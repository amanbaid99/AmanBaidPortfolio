404Found — Website Audit & Outreach Tool

A friend’s web development company needed to find businesses running outdated sites and start a conversation with them. I built the pipeline that does the finding, the auditing and the drafting, then stops before it sends.

Problem

404Found builds websites. Its prospects are businesses whose current site is outdated or underperforming, which is something you can only establish by looking at each one. Finding those businesses, working out what’s wrong with their site, and writing something worth reading is three jobs per prospect, and doing all three by hand is slow.

Approach

I built an n8n workflow that runs the chain end to end. It takes business data from Apollo, looks up each business’s LinkedIn and website, researches the business, generates a website audit report, and drafts a cold outreach email pitching an upgrade.

The draft is where it stops. The 404Found founder reviews and vets every email before it goes anywhere. The tool has no send step of its own. Cold outreach is the company’s reputation arriving in someone’s inbox, and that is not a decision to hand to a workflow.

Outcome

Built to support 404Found’s cold outreach process. I don’t have performance numbers for it. If and when there are some, they belong here rather than an estimate.

Tech used

  • Apollo
  • LLM Workflow Automation

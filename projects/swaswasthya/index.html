Swaswasthya Wellness Platform

A wellness client wanted to be online. What they actually needed was a working product — lead capture, content, an admin panel they could run themselves. I ran the discovery, then designed, coded and deployed the whole thing solo.

Visit swaswasthya.com

Problem

The client came in asking for a website. But the requirements weren't scoped at the outset — there was no clarity on who the site was for, what it needed to do beyond existing, or how they'd keep it alive once it launched. Building straight to the brief would have produced a brochure that went stale in a month.

Approach

I ran discovery workshops directly with the client instead of taking the initial ask at face value. That surfaced the real requirements: they needed inbound leads, not traffic; they needed to publish content without calling a developer; and they needed something to give visitors a reason to engage on the first visit.

That reframing set the scope for everything I built — and moved the project from "a website" to a platform with a growth loop attached.

What I built

  • The full site, solo — designed and coded end to end using AI tools (Claude), from layout through to production code.
  • Supabase-powered admin panel so the client can manage their own content without a developer in the loop.
  • Blog built for organic and AI-driven SEO visibility — the long-term acquisition channel rather than paid spend.
  • Interactive tools — BMI and calorie calculators that give a first-time visitor immediate value, with WhatsApp lead capture attached to convert that attention.
  • PostHog instrumentation tracking real user journeys, so decisions about what to build next come from behaviour rather than opinion.
  • Deployment and infrastructure — running on AWS EC2 with SSL I configured myself.

Outcome

  • Live in production Shipped and running at swaswasthya.com, self-hosted on AWS EC2.
  • Client-operable Content and leads are managed by the client through the admin panel — no ongoing developer dependency.
  • Instrumented from day one PostHog journey tracking means the roadmap is driven by observed behaviour, not guesses.
